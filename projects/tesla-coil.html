ASME UH • PROJECT LEAD

Tesla Coil Project

Led 20 engineering students through the construction and testing of four Tesla coils during two hands-on ASME UH build days.

OVERVIEW

Hands-On Electrical Engineering

The Tesla Coil Project was organized through the University of Houston ASME chapter for students interested in learning how to build and test a working Tesla coil.

Over two build days, students worked together to assemble four Tesla coils while developing hands-on experience in soldering, fabrication, troubleshooting, teamwork, and problem-solving.

MY ROLE

Project Lead

I led the project and instructed approximately 20 engineering students throughout the build.

I helped organize students into teams, guided the construction process, taught hands-on fabrication and soldering techniques, and helped troubleshoot the systems during assembly and testing.

PROJECT

Four Coils in Two Build Days

Students were divided into small teams and worked together to complete each Tesla coil. The project was structured so students could actively participate in the build rather than simply watching a demonstration.
