ASME UH • ENGINEERING PROJECT

Diesel Engine Generator

A student engineering project focused on building a functional diesel-powered generator and demonstrating the conversion of mechanical motion into electrical power.

OVERVIEW

Building a 2.4 kW Generator

The ASME UH Diesel Engine Generator project gave engineering students hands-on experience building a working power-generation system.

The completed generator produced approximately 2.4 kW while demonstrating key engineering concepts in power transmission and converting mechanical motion into electrical power.

MY ROLE

Project Lead

As an ASME UH Projects Officer and project lead, I led and instructed more than 30 engineering students throughout the generator build.

I helped guide students through the project while demonstrating the engineering principles behind power transmission and the conversion of mechanical energy into electrical power.

Functional Power Generation
