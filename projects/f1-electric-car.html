OVERVIEW

Designing and Building a Drivable Electric Formula-Style Car

This ongoing personal project began with the goal of designing and building a full-size, single-seat electric vehicle inspired by Formula 1 cars.

Rather than modifying an existing chassis, I am developing the vehicle from the ground up. The project combines CAD, structural fabrication, welding, electric drivetrain design, steering, braking, bodywork, and system integration.

The current phase of the project focuses on vehicle integration, fabrication, and testing as the individual mechanical and electrical systems are installed and refined.

MY ROLE

Ground-Up Design & Fabrication

I am independently designing and building the vehicle, including the chassis, drivetrain layout, steering system, braking system, electrical integration, and exterior bodywork.

The chassis is fabricated from 1 × 1 inch, 14-gauge square steel tubing and assembled using stick welding. I also designed the drivetrain around a 72 V, 3 kW electric motor driving the rear axle through a chain-drive system.

BODYWORK

Formula-Inspired Exterior Development

The exterior is being developed around the steel chassis using CAD, 3D printing, and fiberglass fabrication techniques.

The goal is to create Formula-inspired bodywork while keeping the structure practical for fabrication and minimizing unnecessary mass.
