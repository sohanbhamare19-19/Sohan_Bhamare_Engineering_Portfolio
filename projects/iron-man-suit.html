PERSONAL PROJECT

Iron Man Suit

A fully functional Iron Man suit inspired by the Avengers films, engineered and built as a personal project in five weeks.

OVERVIEW

Turning a Movie Concept into a Physical Build

This personal project challenged me to take the concept of the Iron Man suit and turn it into a functional physical build.

The suit was designed, fabricated, assembled, tested, and improved over a five-week development period.

MY ROLE

Independent Design & Build

I independently engineered and built the suit from the initial concept through the completed project.

The project required solving design, fabrication, assembly, and integration challenges while working toward a complete functioning suit.
