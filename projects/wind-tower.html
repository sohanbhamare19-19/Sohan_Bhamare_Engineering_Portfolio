UNIVERSITY OF HOUSTON WIND TUNNEL LAB

Wind Tower Instrumentation

Instrumentation built for a 50-meter wind tower to record static pressure at multiple heights over a long-term deployment.

Project Highlights

  • Built static-pressure measurement nodes for a 50-meter tower.
  • Wrote code and completed system wiring.
  • Used CAD and resin printing to create hardware for the measurement nodes.
  • Designed for year-long operation while resisting weather, outages, and environmental noise.
